# Education

This section focuses on education, training, and workforce development in artificial intelligence, engineering, and modern technologies.

Education is closely connected to research, infrastructure, and industry, because trained people are required to build, operate, and improve technological systems.

Education is one of the most important long-term investments for the future of any country. Without proper education systems, it is impossible to build strong technology, industry, research, or innovation ecosystems.

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to:
- Define AI and technology education strategies
- Propose university curriculum improvements
- Design ML engineering and software engineering training programs
- Explore online education and self-learning platforms
- Plan workforce reskilling programs
- Study successful education models from other countries

## Topics in this section

Examples include:
- AI curriculum for universities
- Machine learning engineering training
- Robotics education
- Online education platforms
- Workforce reskilling and upskilling
- Technical schools and vocational training
- Research education programs

## Goal

The long-term goal of this section is to design a **modern education roadmap** that prepares the next generation for AI, robotics, software engineering, and advanced technologies.
