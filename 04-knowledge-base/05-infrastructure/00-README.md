# Infrastructure

This section focuses on technological and digital infrastructure required for AI, software development, research, and modern industry.

Without infrastructure, research and innovation cannot scale. Infrastructure includes computing resources, data platforms, internet infrastructure, and cloud systems.

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to:
- Plan national data centers
- Design GPU clusters for AI research
- Propose cloud platforms
- Define national datasets and data platforms
- Study internet infrastructure improvements
- Build open data platforms
- Support research and startups with infrastructure

## Topics in this section

Examples include:
- Data centers
- GPU clusters
- Cloud platforms
- National datasets
- Open data platforms
- Internet infrastructure
- Research computing infrastructure

## Goal

The long-term goal is to define the infrastructure needed to support:
- AI research
- Startups
- Universities
- Industry
- Government digital services

Infrastructure is the **foundation of a technology-driven economy**.
