# Problems

This section documents major problems, challenges, and structural issues that can potentially be addressed using technology, artificial intelligence, data systems, infrastructure improvements, or policy changes.

Understanding problems clearly is the first step before proposing solutions.  
Good solutions require a deep understanding of the problem, its causes, its impact, and its constraints.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this section is to:
- Document major national and technological challenges
- Provide structured descriptions of problems
- Analyze economic, environmental, and social impact
- Provide background for solution design
- Support research, projects, and reports
- Build a knowledge base of important problems that need long-term solutions

This section should focus on **understanding problems before jumping to solutions**.

---

## What should be documented

Problem documents may include topics such as:
- Water shortage and water management
- Air pollution
- Traffic and transportation
- Agriculture efficiency
- Healthcare systems
- Education systems
- Economy and industry modernization
- Energy management
- Urban planning
- Environmental issues
- Digital infrastructure gaps
- Data availability and data governance
- Workforce and skills gaps

---

## Structure of a Problem Document

Problem documents should ideally include:

1. Problem Description
2. Current Situation
3. Why This Problem Matters
4. Economic Impact
5. Social Impact
6. Environmental Impact (if applicable)
7. Existing Solutions or Attempts
8. Challenges and Constraints
9. Possible Role of Technology / AI
10. References or Examples (if available)

Problem documents should focus on **clear understanding and analysis**, not only opinions.
