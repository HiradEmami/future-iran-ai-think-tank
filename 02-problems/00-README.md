# Problems

---

This section describes major real-world problems and challenges that need to be solved.

The goal of this section is to clearly define problems before proposing solutions.

Each document in this section should try to answer:
- What is the problem?
- Why does it matter?
- What is the current situation?
- What are the consequences if the problem is not solved?
- What data or information is needed to understand the problem better?

Example problem areas:
- Water and environment
- Air pollution
- Traffic and transportation
- Agriculture and food systems
- Healthcare systems
- Education systems
- Economy and industry
- Energy management
- Urban planning and housing
- Infrastructure and logistics

This section focuses on **understanding problems**, not solving them yet.
