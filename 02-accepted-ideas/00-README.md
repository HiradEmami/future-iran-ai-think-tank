# Accepted Ideas

---

This folder contains ideas that have been reviewed and accepted as valuable, feasible, and worth further development.

Ideas in this folder are **approved concepts**, but they are not yet full projects. They are ideas that are ready for planning, team formation, or feasibility studies.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this folder is to:
- Store validated and accepted ideas
- Document ideas that are ready to become projects
- Separate raw ideas from serious initiatives

---

## What should go here

Ideas move here from:

[/01-idea-proposals](/01-idea-proposals) -> [02-accepted-ideas/](/02-accepted-ideas)


An idea should be moved here when:
- The idea is clear and well defined
- The impact is significant
- The idea is technically or economically feasible
- There is interest in developing it further
