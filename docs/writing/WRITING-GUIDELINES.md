# Writing Guidelines

---

## Purpose

This document defines how documents should be written in the Future Iran AI Think Tank repository.  
The goal is to keep the repository structured, readable, consistent, and useful as a long-term knowledge base.

This is not a blog, not a political forum, and not a place for unstructured opinions.  
Documents should be written in a clear, structured, and analytical way.

---

## General Principles

When writing documents:

- Be clear and structured
- Focus on real problems and practical solutions
- Avoid political arguments and ideological discussions
- Use technical, economic, and systems thinking
- Write for long-term usefulness
- Prefer structured sections over long paragraphs
- Use bullet points and headings where possible
- Avoid emotional language
- Support claims with reasoning, data, or examples when possible
- Think like an engineer, researcher, or planner

---

## Writing Style

Please follow these writing style guidelines:

- Use clear section headings
- Use short paragraphs
- Avoid very long sentences
- Prefer simple and clear language
- Define technical terms if necessary
- Write in a neutral and professional tone
- Avoid slang and informal language
- Avoid personal attacks or criticism of individuals
- Focus on systems, problems, and solutions

---

## Idea Documents Structure

Idea cards should follow a structured format.  
Use the Idea Template when creating new ideas.

Typical structure for an idea document:

1. Summary
2. Problem Description
3. Proposed Solution
4. Technology
5. Data Required
6. Infrastructure Required
7. Challenges
8. Potential Impact
9. Related Areas
10. Notes

---

## Problem Documents Structure

Problem documents should focus on clearly explaining a problem.

Suggested structure:

1. Problem Description
2. Current Situation
3. Why This Problem Matters
4. Economic Impact
5. Social Impact
6. Environmental Impact
7. Existing Solutions
8. Challenges
9. Possible Role of AI / Technology
10. References (if available)

---

## Research Documents Structure

Research documents should describe technical areas.

Suggested structure:

1. Research Area Overview
2. Why This Area Matters
3. Current State of the Art
4. Possible Applications
5. Required Data
6. Required Infrastructure
7. Challenges
8. Future Research Directions

---

## Infrastructure Documents Structure

Infrastructure documents should describe systems needed to support AI and technology.

Suggested structure:

1. Overview
2. Why This Infrastructure Is Important
3. Current Situation
4. Proposed Infrastructure
5. Required Technologies
6. Estimated Challenges
7. Long-Term Impact

---

## Education Documents Structure

Education documents should focus on training people and workforce development.

Suggested structure:

1. Overview
2. Current Problems in Education
3. Required Skills for Future
4. Proposed Education Changes
5. Curriculum Ideas
6. Training Programs
7. Long-Term Impact

---

## Regulation Documents Structure

Regulation documents should focus on governance, law, and policy.

Suggested structure:

1. Overview
2. Why Regulation Is Needed
3. Current Challenges
4. Proposed Regulations
5. Risks and Concerns
6. Ethical Considerations
7. Long-Term Impact

---

## General Advice

When writing any document, always try to answer:

- What is the problem?
- Why does it matter?
- How can technology help?
- What is required to implement this?
- What are the challenges?
- What is the impact?

If a document answers these questions clearly, it is a good document.

---

## Final Note

This repository is intended to be a long-term knowledge base and idea platform.  
Clear writing and structured thinking are more valuable than long texts.

Think long-term. Write clearly. Focus on solutions.
