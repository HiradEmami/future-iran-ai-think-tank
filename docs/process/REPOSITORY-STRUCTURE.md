# Repository Structure

---

## Overview

This document explains the structure of the Future Iran AI Think Tank repository and the purpose of each folder and major file.  
The repository is organized around the lifecycle of ideas, projects, knowledge, and reports.

The structure is designed to support:
- Idea generation
- Community discussion and review
- Project development
- Knowledge documentation
- Strategy and report writing
- Long-term research and planning

The repository is not just a collection of documents.  
It is intended to function as an idea platform, knowledge base, and think tank framework.

---

## High-Level Structure

The repository is organized into the following main sections:

```text
01-idea-proposals
02-accepted-ideas
03-projects
04-knowledge-base
05-reports
06-templates
```


In addition, there are governance and documentation files at the root level such as README, CONTRIBUTING, GOVERNANCE, and others.

---

## Idea Lifecycle in Repository

The repository structure follows the lifecycle of an idea:

```text
Discussion → Idea Proposal → Review → Accepted Idea → Project → Knowledge Base → Report
```


Folders are organized to support this lifecycle.

---

## 01-idea-proposals

This folder contains new idea proposals submitted by contributors.

Each idea proposal should:
- Follow the idea template
- Clearly describe a problem and proposed solution
- Include discussion about technology, data, infrastructure, and impact
- Be submitted via Pull Request

Ideas in this folder are considered:
- Draft ideas
- Under discussion
- Not yet accepted by the community

After review and discussion, ideas may be moved to the accepted ideas folder.

---

## 02-accepted-ideas

This folder contains ideas that have been reviewed and accepted by the community.

Accepted ideas are:
- Clearly defined
- Technically reasonable
- Well documented
- Considered valuable for further exploration

Accepted ideas may later become projects, research topics, reports, or knowledge base documents.

---

## 03-projects

This folder contains projects based on accepted ideas.

Projects are more detailed than idea cards and may include:
- System architecture
- Technical design
- Data requirements
- Infrastructure requirements
- Implementation roadmap
- Cost considerations
- Risks and challenges
- Case studies
- Comparisons with other countries or systems

Projects represent deep dives into specific ideas and systems.

---

## 04-knowledge-base

This is the structured knowledge section of the repository.

The knowledge base contains long-term documents organized by topic rather than by individual idea.

The knowledge base currently contains the following sections:

```text
01-vision
02-problems
03-solutions
04-research
05-infrastructure
06-education
07-regulation
```

### 01-vision
Contains long-term vision, mission, principles, and high-level thinking about the future.

### 02-problems
Contains detailed descriptions of major problems and challenges such as:
- Water shortage
- Air pollution
- Traffic
- Agriculture
- Healthcare
- Education
- Economy and industry
- Energy management

These documents focus on understanding problems in depth.

### 03-solutions
Contains documents describing how AI, data science, robotics, and automation can be used to solve problems.

These are more general solution areas, not individual idea cards.

### 04-research
Contains research areas and technical topics such as:
- Persian NLP
- Speech recognition
- Computer vision datasets
- Robotics
- Reinforcement learning
- Control systems and AI
- Climate modeling

This section focuses on technical and research directions.

### 05-infrastructure
Contains documents related to infrastructure required for AI and technology development such as:
- Data centers
- GPU clusters
- Cloud platforms
- National datasets
- Open data platforms
- Internet infrastructure

### 06-education
Contains documents related to education and workforce development such as:
- AI curriculum for universities
- Machine learning engineering training
- Robotics education
- Online education
- Workforce reskilling

### 07-regulation
Contains documents related to governance, law, policy, and ethics such as:
- Data privacy
- AI regulation
- Autonomous systems regulation
- AI and jobs
- AI ethics

---

## 05-reports

This folder contains reports and strategy documents.

Reports may include:
- Technology strategies
- Infrastructure strategies
- Education reform proposals
- Industry roadmaps
- Policy recommendations
- White papers
- Long-term planning documents

Reports are typically more formal and structured documents compared to idea cards or knowledge base articles.

---

## 06-templates

This folder contains templates for writing documents in a consistent format.

Templates include:
- Idea template
- Project template
- Problem template
- Research template
- Infrastructure template
- Education template
- Regulation template
- Report template

Contributors should use templates when creating new documents.

---

## Root-Level Files

Important files at the root of the repository include:

- **README.md**  
  Overview of the project and how to contribute.


- **CONTRIBUTING.md**  
  Explains how contributors should contribute to the project.


- **CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md**  
  Defines expected behavior and community standards.


- **GOVERNANCE.md**  
  Describes how decisions are made and how the project is managed.


- **ROADMAP.md**  
  Describes long-term plans and future directions for the project.


- **PROJECT-PHILOSOPHY.md**  
  Explains the philosophy and mindset behind the project.


- **IDEA-LIFECYCLE.md**  
  Explains how ideas move through the project.


- **WRITING-GUIDELINES.md**  
  Explains how documents should be written.


- **SECURITY.md**  
  Security policy for the repository.


- **LICENSE**  
  Open-source license.

---

## Summary

The repository is organized around the lifecycle of ideas and the development of knowledge and strategy.

In simple terms:

```text
Idea Proposals → Accepted Ideas → Projects → Knowledge Base → Reports
```


This structure is designed to support long-term collaboration, structured thinking, and systematic development of ideas, research, and strategies.
