# Governance

## Project Governance Overview

Future Iran AI Think Tank is an open, community-driven project.  
The goal of governance is to ensure that the project remains organized, respectful, constructive, and focused on its mission.

This document describes how decisions are made, how contributions are reviewed, and how the project is managed over time.

---

## Project Goals

The project aims to:

- Identify major real-world problems
- Explore how AI, robotics, and technology can help solve them
- Build a structured knowledge base
- Encourage research and technical discussion
- Propose infrastructure and education strategies
- Think long-term about technology and development
- Create a collaborative platform for ideas and research

---

## Roles

### Contributors
Contributors are individuals who add content, ideas, documents, or participate in discussions.  
Anyone can become a contributor by submitting issues, documents, or pull requests.

### Maintainers
Maintainers review contributions, organize the repository, guide discussions, and ensure the project stays aligned with its goals.

### Community Members
Community members may participate in discussions, provide feedback, suggest ideas, and help improve documents even if they do not submit pull requests.

---
## Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing Pull Requests
- Maintaining repository structure
- Ensuring content quality and clarity
- Enforcing the Code of Conduct
- Guiding the direction of the project
- Organizing documents and categories
- Managing discussions and issues

Maintainers are not owners of ideas — they are responsible for keeping the project organized, constructive, and aligned with its mission.

---

## Decision Making

Most decisions in this project should be made through discussion and consensus.

Typical process:

1. A contributor proposes an idea, document, or change.
2. Discussion happens in Issues, Pull Requests, or Discussions.
3. Contributors provide feedback and suggestions.
4. Maintainers review the proposal.
5. If the proposal aligns with the project goals, it is accepted and merged.

Maintainers may make final decisions when necessary to keep the project organized and aligned with its mission.

---

## Types of Decisions

Decisions in this project may include:

- Repository structure changes
- Acceptance or rejection of documents
- Creation of new sections or categories
- Project roadmap changes
- Governance updates

Small decisions can be made by maintainers.  
Larger structural decisions should be discussed with the community before implementation.

---

## Content Guidelines

Content added to this repository should generally be:

- Technical
- Research-oriented
- Engineering-focused
- Solution-oriented
- Long-term oriented
- Structured and clear
- Respectful and professional

Content that is purely political, ideological, insulting, or unrelated to the project goals may be removed.

---

## Conflict Resolution

If disagreements occur:

1. Focus on the problem, not the person.
2. Use technical reasoning and evidence.
3. Be respectful and professional.
4. Try to reach a constructive compromise.
5. If needed, maintainers will make the final decision.

The goal is not to win arguments, but to **build knowledge and good ideas together**.

## Project Evolution

This project is intended to grow over time and may evolve into:

- A public knowledge base
- A think tank
- A research community
- A collection of technical reports and white papers
- A technology roadmap
- A collaboration platform
- A source of startup and research ideas

The governance model may evolve as the project grows.
