# Reports

---

This folder contains reports, studies, analyses, white papers, and research documents produced from projects, research, or strategic studies.

Reports are more formal documents that document and summarize:
- Research results
- Project outcomes
- Policy recommendations
- Feasibility studies
- Strategic analyses
- Technical reports

---

## Purpose

The goal of this folder is to:
- Store official reports and documents
- Document results of projects
- Provide decision-making material
- Share knowledge and findings

---

## Examples of reports

- AI strategy reports
- Infrastructure feasibility studies
- Education reform reports
- Industry analysis
- Technology roadmap reports
- Research summaries
