# Idea Lifecycle

## Overview

This document describes how ideas move through the Future Iran AI Think Tank project.

The repository is idea-driven.  
Ideas are the starting point for research, projects, reports, and strategy discussions.

An idea typically moves through several stages before becoming a project, report, or part of the knowledge base.

---

## Idea Lifecycle Stages

The typical lifecycle of an idea in this project is:

1. Discussion
2. Idea Proposal
3. Review and Feedback
4. Accepted Idea
5. Project / Deep Dive
6. Knowledge Base Contributions
7. Report / Strategy Document

---

## Stage 1 – Discussion

At this stage, ideas are informal.

People may:
- Open a GitHub Discussion
- Open an Issue
- Talk about a problem
- Suggest a possible solution
- Ask questions
- Share articles or research

This stage is for brainstorming and early thinking.

---

## Stage 2 – Idea Proposal

When an idea becomes clearer, it should be written as an Idea Card using the idea template.

The contributor should:
1. Fork the repository
2. Create a new idea file in `01-idea-proposals`
3. Follow the idea template
4. Open a Pull Request
5. Describe the idea in the Pull Request

This is the formal idea proposal stage.

---

## Stage 3 – Review and Feedback

The community reviews the idea:

- Is the problem real?
- Is the solution realistic?
- Is AI actually useful here?
- What data is required?
- What infrastructure is required?
- Are there similar systems in other countries?
- What are the risks?
- What is the potential impact?

Discussion happens in:
- Pull Request comments
- GitHub Discussions
- Issues

The idea may be updated and improved based on feedback.

---

## Stage 4 – Accepted Idea

If the idea is clear, useful, and well-structured, it can be accepted.

Accepted ideas are moved from:

```text
01-idea-proposals → 02-accepted-ideas
```


Accepted ideas represent ideas that the community considers valuable and worth further exploration.

---

## Stage 5 – Project / Deep Dive

Some accepted ideas may become projects.

Projects involve:
- Detailed technical analysis
- Architecture design
- Data requirements
- Infrastructure planning
- Cost estimation
- Implementation roadmap
- Research requirements

These are stored in:

```text
03-projects
```


Projects are more detailed than idea cards.

---

## Stage 6 – Knowledge Base Contributions

Ideas and projects may lead to new documents in the knowledge base:

- Problems
- Research areas
- Infrastructure needs
- Education changes
- Regulation and policy

These are stored in:

```text
04-knowledge-base
```


The knowledge base grows over time as ideas and projects are explored.

---

## Stage 7 – Reports and Strategy Documents

When enough knowledge and analysis exist, reports or strategy documents can be written.

Examples:
- National AI Infrastructure Strategy
- Water Management AI Strategy
- AI Education Reform Proposal
- Robotics Industry Roadmap
- Smart Cities Strategy

These are stored in:

```text
05-reports
```

---

## Summary Diagram

Idea lifecycle:
```text
Discussion
    ↓
Idea Proposal
    ↓
Review / Feedback
    ↓
Accepted Idea
    ↓
Project / Deep Dive
    ↓
Knowledge Base
    ↓

```

This lifecycle helps the project stay organized and structured.
