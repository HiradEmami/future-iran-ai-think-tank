# Roadmap

---

## Overview

This roadmap outlines the long-term development direction of the Future Iran AI Think Tank project.

The goal of this project is not short-term software development, but the gradual creation of a structured knowledge base, research direction platform, and collaborative environment focused on artificial intelligence, robotics, data science, infrastructure, education, and technology-driven solutions.

This roadmap is expected to evolve over time as the project grows and the community expands.

---

## Phase 1 – Repository Foundation

**Goal:** Establish the structure and core documents of the project.

Main tasks:
- Create repository structure
- Write README
- Add Code of Conduct
- Add Contributing Guidelines
- Add Governance document
- Define repository sections (Vision, Problems, Solutions, Research, Infrastructure, Education, Regulation, Discussions)
- Create document templates
- Define writing structure for contributions

Outcome:
A well-structured repository ready for contributions.

---

## Phase 2 – Problem Documentation

**Goal:** Identify and document major real-world problems and challenges.

Focus areas may include:
- Water and environment
- Energy systems
- Transportation and traffic
- Agriculture and food systems
- Healthcare systems
- Education systems
- Economy and industry
- Urban planning and housing
- Infrastructure and logistics
- Digital infrastructure and data systems

Outcome:
A structured collection of clearly defined problems and challenges.

---

## Phase 3 – AI and Technology Solutions

**Goal:** Explore how AI, robotics, data science, and automation can help solve the documented problems.

Possible topics:
- Machine learning and prediction systems
- Optimization and resource allocation
- Computer vision systems
- Natural language processing
- Robotics and automation
- Decision support systems
- Simulation and modeling
- Digital platforms and data infrastructure
- Smart cities and intelligent infrastructure

Outcome:
A structured set of solution ideas linked to real-world problems.

---

## Phase 4 – Research Directions

**Goal:** Identify important research and technical areas for long-term development.

Examples:
- Persian NLP
- Speech recognition
- Computer vision datasets
- Robotics and autonomous systems
- Reinforcement learning
- Control systems and AI
- Climate and environmental modeling
- Industrial AI
- Smart infrastructure
- Data platforms and large-scale systems

Outcome:
A collection of research topics and technical directions.

## Phase 5 – Infrastructure and Technology Strategy

**Goal:** Discuss the infrastructure required to support AI and technology development.

Topics may include:
- Data centers
- GPU clusters and compute infrastructure

- National datasets
- Open data platforms

- Sensors and IoT systems
- Research labs and technology parks

- Digital public infrastructure

Outcome:
Infrastructure strategy documents and ideas.

---

## Phase 6 – Education and Workforce Development

**Goal:** Explore how education systems and workforce training should evolve.

Topics:
- AI curriculum for universities
- Machine learning engineering programs

- Technical schools and vocational training
- Online education platforms
- Reskilling workforce for automation
- Interdisciplinary education (AI + engineering + economics + policy)
- Research programs and graduate education
- Open educational resources

Outcome:
Education and workforce development proposals.

---

## Phase 7 – Regulation, Governance, and Ethics

**Goal:** Discuss regulation and governance related to AI and technology.

- Data privacy

- Open data policies

Outcome:
Policy and governance discussion documents.

---

## Phase 8 – Publications and Reports

**Goal:** Convert collected knowledge into structured reports and documents.

Possible outputs:
- White papers
- Technical reports
- Technology roadmaps
- Research surveys
- Policy suggestion documents

Formal documents and publications.

---

## Long-Term Vision

If the project grows over time, it may evolve into:

- A public knowledge base
- A think tank
- A research community
- A technology strategy platform
- A collection of technical reports and white papers
- A source of startup and research ideas
- A collaboration platform for engineers and researchers
- A long-term technology roadmap initiative

This roadmap is expected to evolve as the project grows and new contributors join.
